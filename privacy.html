A note on your information

Privacy policy.

Written in plain language by Silva and Kamelia. We have tried to keep it short and honest — the way a careful friend would tell you what they do with what you share.

Last updated · May MMXXVI

Who we are

Star Life is a small astrology practice operated by Silva and Kamelia. We can be reached at hello@star-life.co. This policy describes how we handle personal information when you visit star-life.co or work with us as a client.

What we collect

When you book an intro call

Through our Zoom Scheduler embed, we collect: your name, your email address, the service you are interested in, your preferred astrologer (Silva, Kamelia, or no preference), your language preference, and whatever you choose to share in the "what brings you here?" field. This information arrives in our Zoom and email inboxes.

When you become a client

For astrology services, we additionally collect: your date of birth, your time of birth (if known), and your place of birth. These three coordinates are essential — without them, no chart can be calculated. We also keep notes from our sessions with you for the duration of our work together, and recordings of your sessions which we deliver to you and retain for reference.

When you simply visit the site

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages and serves fonts from Google Fonts. Both services log basic technical information (your IP address, browser type, referring page) for security and performance — this is standard for nearly all websites on the internet. We do not run analytics scripts, advertising trackers, or cookies of our own. We do not know who visits our site unless you reach out to us.

What we do with it

We use the information you share with us only to:

  • Schedule and conduct your sessions
  • Prepare and deliver your reading
  • Respond to your messages and questions
  • Send you the recordings and notes from our sessions
  • Keep our own records of clients we are working with

We do not sell your information. We do not share it with marketers, data brokers, or anyone else for any commercial purpose. We do not include you in mailing lists or newsletters without your explicit, prior consent.

Who else sees it

The few third parties who process information on our behalf:

  • Zoom — for video sessions, scheduling, and recordings. Zoom has its own privacy policy at explore.zoom.us/en/privacy.
  • PayPal — for payment processing. We never see or store your full payment details. PayPal's privacy policy applies to that part of the transaction.
  • Email provider — our email inbox is hosted by a standard provider whose privacy practices align with industry norms.
  • Google Fonts — the typefaces on this website are served from Google. Google may log technical information when fonts load.
  • GitHub — this website is hosted on GitHub Pages.

Beyond these necessary processors, no one sees your information. We do not discuss specific clients with other clients. We do not share charts, notes, or recordings with anyone other than you.

How long we keep it

We keep your chart data, session notes, and recordings for as long as we are working with you, and for a reasonable period afterward — usually up to three years — so that we can support you if you return for a consultation. After that period, or sooner if you ask us, we delete what we hold.

Booking inquiries that do not lead to a session are deleted within twelve months.

Your rights

If you are in the European Union, the United Kingdom, California, or any jurisdiction with similar data-protection laws, you have the right to:

  • Ask us what we hold about you
  • Ask us to correct anything inaccurate
  • Ask us to delete what we hold (subject to any reasonable record-keeping requirements)
  • Ask us to send you a copy of what we hold
  • Withdraw your consent at any time, for anything we are doing with your consent

To exercise any of these rights, please email hello@star-life.co. We will respond within thirty days, usually sooner. We will not charge you for any of these requests.

Cookies

This website does not set its own cookies. The Zoom Scheduler embed on our booking page may set cookies when you interact with it — those are governed by Zoom's privacy policy, linked above. We do not run Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, or any other tracking script.

Children

Our services are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under sixteen. If you believe a minor has shared information with us, please contact us so we can remove it.

Changes to this policy

If we change anything substantive, we will update the date at the top of this page and, where appropriate, tell our active clients directly. The current version is always the one published here.

How to reach us

For anything related to your privacy or this policy, please write to hello@star-life.co. We read every message ourselves.
