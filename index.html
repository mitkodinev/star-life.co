I.

Why astrology

"To know yourself is the beginning of all wisdom — and the chart you were born under is one of the oldest mirrors we have.

In essence

Astrology is not prophecy. It is not a list of things that will happen. It is a language — old, careful, and patient — for understanding the patterns you were born into and the rhythms you live within. Your chart is a portrait of a single moment in the sky, and that moment belongs to no one but you.

Used well, astrology helps you see the parts of yourself you have always sensed but never quite named. It reveals where your energy moves easily, where it meets resistance, and which seasons of your life are calling for boldness — and which are quietly asking you to wait. It will not make your decisions. It will help you make them with more of yourself in the room.

II.

Our approach

Every reading is the work of two astrologers, drawn out across a week, never rushed, never delivered alone.

No. 01

You reach out

A short conversation about what brings you here — what you are hoping to understand, decide, or carry differently.

No. 02

We study the chart

Across roughly a week, both of us read your chart in two professional softwares, using several traditional methods, and we consult each other on what we find.

No. 03

We meet, together

Three one-hour sessions over Zoom — both Silva and Kamelia present. We listen, you ask, we explore. The recording is yours to keep.

No. 04

You carry it forward

A reading is not the end of a conversation. We remain available — in moments of decision, doubt, or simple wondering.

The methods

We work with Time Nomad and Zet — two of the most respected astrology platforms in serious practice — and combine their analyses with traditional methods we have refined over more than fifteen years between us. Every reading is cross-checked between Silva and Kamelia before it reaches you. The slowness is the point.
