I.

Why astrology

“To know yourself is the beginning of all wisdom — and the chart you were born under is one of the oldest mirrors we have.

In essence

Astrology is not prophecy. It is not a list of things that will happen. It is a language — old, careful, and patient — for understanding the patterns you were born into and the rhythms you live within. Your chart is a portrait of a single moment in the sky, and that moment belongs to no one but you.

Used well, astrology helps you see the parts of yourself you have always sensed but never quite named. It reveals where your energy moves easily, where it meets resistance, and which seasons of your life are calling for boldness — and which are quietly asking you to wait. It will not make your decisions. It will help you make them with more of yourself in the room.

II.

Our approach

Every reading is the work of two astrologers, drawn out across a week, never rushed, never delivered alone.

  1. No. 01

    You reach out

    A short conversation about what brings you here — what you are hoping to understand, decide, or carry differently.

  2. No. 02

    We study the chart

    Across roughly a week, both of us read your chart in two professional softwares, using several traditional methods, and we consult each other on what we find.

  3. No. 03

    We meet, together

    Three one-hour sessions over Zoom — both Silva and Kamelia present. We listen, you ask, we explore. The recording is yours to keep.

  4. No. 04

    You carry it forward

    A reading is not the end of a conversation. We remain available — in moments of decision, doubt, or simple wondering.

The methods

We work with Time Nomad and Zet — two of the most respected astrology platforms in serious practice — and combine their analyses with traditional methods we have refined over more than fifteen years between us. Every reading is cross-checked between Silva and Kamelia before it reaches you. The slowness is the point.

01 I don't know anything about astrology. Is that a problem?

Not at all. Many of our clients come to us without ever having read a horoscope. We will explain everything we share — no jargon, no assumed knowledge, no judgement. The vocabulary will arrive naturally as we talk, the way a friend would explain something they love.

If you'd like to look ahead, our vocabulary page gathers the terms we use most often.

02 I don't know my exact birth time. Can you still read my chart?

Yes — but with a gentle caveat. The exact birth time determines your Ascendant and the placement of the houses, which carry significant meaning in a complete reading. Without it, much of the chart still reads clearly (the Sun, Moon, and planetary aspects do not depend on it), but the angular detail is softer.

If you can find your birth certificate, hospital record, or a parent's memory, we recommend it. If not, we have techniques (called chart rectification) that can estimate a time from the events of your life. We will discuss this on the intro call.

03 Do you predict the future?

No — and we are careful about this. Astrology, in our practice, is a language for understanding patterns and timing, not for foretelling specific events. We can speak to the seasons of your life — when energy is gathering, when caution is wise, when a window is opening — but the choices remain yours.

If you encounter an astrologer who promises specific predictions ("you will meet someone in March"), we would gently encourage you to be skeptical. The stars suggest; they do not dictate.

04 Why does a reading take a whole week?

Because a chart deserves it. Both Silva and Kamelia read your chart independently using two professional software platforms, then we compare notes, discuss what we each saw, and refine the reading until we agree on what is most worth saying to you. A rushed reading is a shallow reading. The slowness is the practice.

05 How are the sessions held? Are they recorded?

All sessions are held privately over Zoom. Each session is recorded and the recording is yours to keep — to return to, to think alongside, to share with no one but yourself. We find that clients revisit their recordings months and sometimes years later, hearing things they could not yet hear the first time.

06 Can I bring questions about a specific decision?

Yes — and we encourage it. The third session of an Initial Reading is reserved for your questions. Career, relationship, timing, health, family — anything that is alive for you. We will look at what the chart and the current sky have to say, and we will be honest with you about what we see.

07 Do you work with people of any faith — or no faith?

Yes. Astrology, as we practice it, is not a religion. It does not ask you to believe anything. It is closer to a language or a craft — a way of paying careful attention to patterns. Clients of every faith, and clients of no faith, have found something useful here.

08 Is what I share confidential?

Absolutely. Everything you share with us — your chart, your questions, your circumstances — is held in confidence between Silva, Kamelia, and you. We do not discuss clients with other clients, ever. Our privacy policy describes how we handle your personal information in detail.

09 How do payments work? Do you offer refunds?

Payment is handled through PayPal once your first session is scheduled — never before. For Initial Readings, full payment is requested before the first session begins. For Consultations and Tarot Readings, payment is settled at the time of booking.

If you feel a reading did not serve you, please reach out. We have not yet had a client ask for a refund, but we would much rather have an honest conversation about what was missing than have you walk away unhappy.

10 In what languages do you work?

Readings are offered in English and Bulgarian. Please mention your preferred language when you book the intro call. If you are most comfortable in another language, please reach out — we will do our best to find a way.
